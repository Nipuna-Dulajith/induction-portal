📖 Supplier User Guide
Step-by-step instructions for new suppliers
1
How to Register
▼

If this is your first time, you need to create an account:

  1. Click the Register tab on the left
  2. Enter your Company Name exactly as it appears on your trade license
  3. Choose a Password (minimum 6 characters)
  4. Confirm the password and click Create Account
  5. Once registered, switch to the Supplier Login tab to log in
⚠️ Each company name can only be registered once. Make sure the name is spelled correctly.
2
How to Login
▼

To access your document portal:

  1. Click the Supplier Login tab
  2. Enter your registered Company Name
  3. Enter your Password
  4. Click Login to Portal
💡 If you forgot your password, click the Forgot Password tab to request a reset code from the admin.
3
Download & Use Templates
▼

After logging in, you will see a Download Required Templates section with two official templates:

📄
Undertaking Letter Template
Word Document (.docx) — Fill in company and employee details, sign, and upload
📊
Attendance & HSE Induction Template
Excel Spreadsheet (.xlsx) — Fill in all employee details with their ID numbers
  1. Click the download button for each template
  2. Fill in all required information
  3. Save the completed file
  4. Upload it back in the document section
4
Upload Company Documents
▼

The following 7 mandatory company documents must be uploaded:

📋Trade License + Expiry Date
📝Undertaking Letter
📄Contract Agreement / LPO
🪪Signing Authority's Emirates ID + Expiry Date
🏥Health Insurance Policy (with employee list) + Expiry Date
🛡Workmen Compensation Policy (with employee list) + Expiry Date
📊Attendance & HSE Induction Excel
  1. Click ⬆ Upload next to each document
  2. For documents with expiry dates, set the date before or after uploading
  3. To replace a document, click ↑ Replace
  4. To remove a document, click 🗑 Remove
✅ The progress bar at the top shows how many mandatory documents have been uploaded.
5
Add Employees, Flag Men, Operators & Drivers
▼

Under Workforce Documents there are 4 tabs — Employees, Flag Men, Operators, Drivers. For each tab:

  1. Enter the employee's Full Name and Employee ID Number
  2. Click + Add to add them to the list
  3. Click 📂 Manage Docs next to the employee to upload their documents

Each employee requires:

🛂Colored Passport Copy
🪪Colored Emirates ID
✅Valid Work Permit (MOHRE)
🖼Passport Size Photo (JPG, white background)
🏥Occupational Health Insurance
6
Add Operators & Their Documents
▼

Under Workforce Documents → Operators tab:

  1. Enter the operator's Full Name and Employee ID
  2. Click + Add then 📂 Manage Docs

Each operator requires 11 documents:

🛂Colored Passport Copy
🪪Colored Emirates ID
✅Valid Work Permit (MOHRE)
🖼Passport Size Photo (JPG, white background)
🏥Occupational Health Insurance
🏗Equipment Third Party Certificate (Enas approved)
📋Equipment License
🔧Equipment Maintenance Record
🎓Operator Third Party Training Certificate (Actvet approved)
🪪Operator License
🩺Medical Examination Report (OSHAD)
7
Check Document Status & Comments
▼

After the admin reviews your documents, each document will show one of these statuses:

✓ Approved — Document accepted, no action needed
✗ Rejected — Document rejected, re-upload required
⏳ Under Review — Admin is reviewing the document
📋 Check the Admin Comments section at the top of your portal for feedback and instructions from the admin.
