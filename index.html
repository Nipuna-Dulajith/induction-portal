📖 Supplier Guide
1
Register & Login
▼
  1. Click Register tab and create your account with your company name
  2. Login using your company name and password
  3. If not assigned to a project yet, contact the site admin
2
Download Templates
▼

After login, download the required templates:

  • 📄 Undertaking Letter Template (.docx)
  • 📊 Attendance & HSE Induction Template (.xlsx)
Fill in the templates and upload the completed files back in the document section.
3
Upload Company Documents
▼

Upload all required company documents for your assigned project. These are shared across all induction sessions.

⚠ Company documents with expiry dates must have their expiry date set before saving.
4
Add Induction Sessions
▼

Create a session for each induction date. Each session has its own group of workers.

  1. Click + Add Session and select the date
  2. Open the session to add workers
  3. Each worker can only be in one session per project
💡 If the same worker needs to redo induction, add them in a new session and upload fresh documents.
5
Add Workers & Upload Documents
▼

Inside each session, add workers by category:

  • 👷 Employees
  • 🚩 Special Doc. Req. Personnel
  • 🏗 Operators
  • 🚗 Drivers

Upload each worker's required documents. A worker cannot appear in multiple sessions in the same project.
